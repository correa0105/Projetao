## Prompt do ativo selecionado

Create ONE production-ready RPG navigation ICON SPRITE ATLAS, exactly 3 columns by 2 rows of equal square cells, landscape 3:2 aspect ratio, transparent background. Reference image is the supplied parchment game-icon sheet; use it as close visual guidance for the BOOK, SWORD and MAP forms and hand-drawn outlines. No labels, no paper backdrop, no rectangular tile backgrounds, no frames, no watermarks, no text. Every icon centered precisely in its own grid cell with 12% transparent padding on all sides, consistent scale and consistent light from upper left. Do NOT draw grid lines.
Six icons in EXACT reading order:
TOP LEFT: the reference's medieval hanging FLAG pennant with two finial posts, cords, curved fabric and a simple wolf insignia.
TOP MIDDLE: the reference's ornate SHIELD with angular rim, a subtle engraved heraldic animal, three-quarter view, well proportioned.
TOP RIGHT: the reference's distinctive broad leaf-shaped SWORD angled upper-left tip to lower-right pommel, thick curved blade, central bevel, ornate crossguard and short wrapped handle. Blade and handle on one perfectly straight diagonal axis. Match the reference sword silhouette closely.
BOTTOM LEFT: a rounded medieval COIN POUCH with gathered neck, looped drawstring and a few small coins, in the same drawing style.
BOTTOM MIDDLE: the reference's rolled parchment MAP with curled cylindrical scroll ends and faint route marks, tilted slightly, dimensional folded corners.
BOTTOM RIGHT: the reference's thick leather-bound BOOK with two horizontal straps and metal clasps, three-quarter view, visible curved spine and page edges.
Art direction: beautiful hand-inked fantasy inventory illustrations, bold clean dark-brown outer contours, finer confident interior engraved linework, warm antique parchment/tan and muted copper fills, shallow 2.5D relief through selective ochre shadows and cream edge highlights. Mostly contour-focused like the reference, not glossy gold 3D metal, not flat geometric icons, not photorealistic. Clear beautiful silhouettes visible at 48px. Warm muted copper/parchment colors suitable on midnight-blue UI. Keep transparency real around each object; no colored glow. Reference banners and advertising are not part of the artwork.
