## Prompt original

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original panoramic background art for the Marca do Norte medieval tabletop RPG guild web application, used in a dashboard banner and login. Create a breathtaking restrained painterly fantasy environment, cinematic wide landscape 3:2 or wider. Snow-clad jagged northern mountains, a monumental weathered medieval stone keep and watchtower perched on the right-hand mountain ridge, tiny warm amber-lit windows, a winding pass through misty slate-blue valleys below. Twilight navy blue sky, moonlit ice, weathered copper accents, muted ivory snow. Rich hand-painted book-cover quality, atmosphere and careful composition, realistic proportions, no cartoon outlines. Keep left half darker and relatively quiet with layered distant mountain silhouettes to permit readable overlaid interface text; strongest fortress silhouette and light on right two thirds. No green hue, no neon, no purple aurora, no people in foreground, no dragon, no typography, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no UI. Elegant, ancient, adventurous, strong sense of place.
