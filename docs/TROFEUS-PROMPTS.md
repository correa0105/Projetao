## Prompt dos troféus
Create ONE production game sprite atlas, 4 columns x 2 rows equal cells, transparent background. Seven distinct medieval fantasy shelf trophy OBJECTS (not medals, not badges, no enclosing frames). Row1: upright closed aged leather book bronze corners; plump leather money pouch with few gold coins at its base; rolled mission parchment standing in small bronze holder; battered northern knight helmet on short stone display foot. Row2: open storyteller tome with feather quill on low bookrest; small open treasure chest filled gold; northern silver crown on low dark stone cushion/plinth; last cell EMPTY. Consistent realistic painterly medieval RPG inventory art, dark walnut leather, aged bronze, muted silver, warm subtle highlights, strong readable silhouettes, detailed but restrained. Front view slightly seeing tops, all fully visible isolated, NO text numbers labels scene or ground. Each object centered in its cell, occupies 75 percent cell width maximum, base exactly 90 percent down its cell, height about 80 percent of cell. Objects must look like physical collectible rewards placed on a shelf. No halo, no sparkle, no circular medallions. Genuinely transparent surrounding pixels.

## Prompt da madeira
Seamless dark aged walnut wood texture for a medieval fantasy game's menu panels. Flat straight-on orthographic surface, fine long horizontal wood grain, subtle knots and worn fibers, dark neutral brown umber, low contrast. No red tint, no stone, no marble, no parchment, no objects, no border, no writing, no light spot or perspective. Realistic wood with restrained fine detail, suitable behind readable text.
