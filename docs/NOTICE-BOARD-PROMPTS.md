## Cenário — prompt final
Wide 3:2 realistic painterly dark medieval fantasy RPG environment for website background. Natural quiet CORNER of an old village, NOT a symmetrical square. An old large notice board is mounted flush against a weathered stone-and-timber tavern wall on the LEFT and middle part of frame, under its small crooked wooden rain awning. Board is physically nailed into wall with believable contact shadows, NO freestanding legs, NO isolated structure in the middle of a street. The wall occupies left 72% of image; the right 28% opens into a narrow cobbled lane receding past a warm-lit cottage, stacked barrels and modest hanging lantern. Asymmetrical, lived-in composition, eye-level camera, board face almost front-on with only slight perspective so papers are readable. Board face spans x16% to66%, y28% to76%, believable real scale. Many small torn faded paper notices, ink sketches, wax remnants clustered around board outer edges; leave three broad vertical adjacent areas of BARE WOOD in the central 60% of board face for interactive notice overlays. Above central areas leave a little wood for handwritten headings. No legible words anywhere. Detailed aged wood grain, moss at foot of wall, cobbles, hanging herbs, iron hooks, warm lantern and cool twilight, restrained neutral earth palette, strong atmospheric depth, beautifully illustrated like premium medieval RPG camp or library backgrounds. Top 15% dark quiet wall/roof for website header. The crucial requirement is environmental plausibility: notice board BELONGS TO THE TAVERN WALL at a village corner, not a standalone foreground prop.
