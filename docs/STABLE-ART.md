# Artes do Estábulo

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada imagegen. Arquivos em `public/stable/`; alpha preservado nas montarias e na tratadora. Retratos de cabeça usam enquadramento CSS da mesma arte individual.

## paddock

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: full-width background for a realistic medieval fantasy RPG stable page. Wide cinematic landscape 16:9, detailed painterly realism, dusk with warm late afternoon light from upper left. Large EMPTY lush natural muted green grassy paddock fills lower two thirds, level ground, clear central space for separately composited horses. Rustic timber and stone stable with warm lamps along upper left background, low wooden fence recedes to distant pine forest and misty hills, blue grey sky. No animals, no people, no text, no UI. Natural proportions, grass texture, restrained warm copper and forest green palette, sophisticated realistic game environment.
