## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: high quality panoramic background illustration for the entrance of a medieval RPG web application, Alvorada Cinzenta.
Primary request: an original fantasy WORLD MAP with large coherent continental landmasses and sharply legible geographic regions, inspired by the elevated hand-painted relief and atmospheric world-map presentation of the Runeterra interactive atlas. Invent your own geography.
Composition: wide 16:9 panoramic aerial atlas, near top-down with slight dimensional tilt, entire world visible. TWO massive continental landmasses taking up most of the image and one modest eastern peninsula; only two or three tiny offshore islands, NOT an archipelago, NOT a scattering of rocky islands. A broad northern continent spans upper left to upper right with snowy alpine mountains along its northern rim, sweeping moss-green forests and broad valleys below. A large southern continent with muted ochre plateau and desert basin, folded ridges and a river delta. Dark slate-blue inland sea and a calm narrow central strait provide space for a separately rendered centered title. Regions are large readable connected expanses separated naturally by mountain ranges, rivers and escarpments, not political border lines. Continuous land with extensive inland terrain.
Style: polished painted 3D relief fantasy cartography, premium strategy-game world atlas. Bold geographic shapes, individually sculpted mountain ridges, delicate forest texture, fine coastlines, dimensional cliffs, tasteful ocean contour currents, distant atmospheric falloff. High detail but strong large-scale legibility, not photoreal satellite.
Palette and lighting: dark restrained moss greens, muted olive woods, weathered warm ochre, charcoal slate ocean, cool desaturated ivory snow. Subtle warm directional lighting from upper left. Rich dark midtones with enough terrain contrast to read. No saturated emerald, cyan, neon, purple or candy colors.
Constraints: original geography, no copied League of Legends locations, no logos, no text, no labels, no icons, no UI, no frames. No baked foreground fog banks because the webpage animates its own clouds. Edge atmosphere only. NOT a globe, no spherical projection, no horizon.
