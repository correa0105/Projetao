## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: high quality widescreen background illustration for the fantasy tabletop RPG portal Alvorada Cinzenta. Create an original premium fantasy world map, very similar in presentation to the League of Legends Runeterra interactive map landing screen: near overhead camera with shallow three-dimensional sculpted terrain relief, painterly realistic miniature mountains and river valleys, not a flat parchment chart. Exactly THREE major landmasses, not scattered archipelago: one very large northern-western continent spanning the top left and center with snow-covered craggy mountains in the far north, muted moss forests and sweeping valleys toward its southern shores; a large southern island with an arid plateau, canyons and cool dark green foothills; and a smaller distinct northeastern island with mountainous interior. Broad dark slate navy sea channels clearly separate the three landmasses. Carefully designed original irregular coastline geometry, geological continuity, sharply detailed relief with cast shadows, natural rivers. Center composition balanced and relatively calm for a large centered HTML title; terrain occupies central 80 percent of the scene, ocean and thin silver-white atmospheric haze around edges. Cool subdued gray dawn lighting, moss green not vivid, stone grays, muted sand, white snow, ocean blue-black. Rich readable terrain and dimensionality, polished fantasy strategy-game atlas quality. Wide 16:9 landscape, 2560x1440 or best available quality. NO TEXT at all, no lettering, no compass, no buttons, no labels, no border, no UI, no symbols, no watermark, no characters, no floating sky islands. No extra little islands. Fog is light and peripheral because the webpage will animate its own fog. Do not reproduce exact Runeterra geography; create original world with the same cinematic map atmosphere.
